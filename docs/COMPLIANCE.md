# Privacy and store-compliance notes (India) — review before launch

> The public Privacy Policy, Terms and Refund Policy are generated from `app/js/legal-text.js` for both the app and crawlable pages. They describe current code paths but are not legal advice. Have counsel confirm the data-fiduciary/legal-entity name, designated grievance contact, lawful basis, retention period and processor terms before launch. The studio profile currently supplies only a display name, email and postal address; do not present those as a registered legal entity unless verified.

## Data the application actually processes

- **Account and identity:** name, email, a phone-derived internal placeholder email on SMS-only accounts, verified phone number for SMS sign-in, scrypt password hash, social-provider subject IDs, profile names/colours/Kids flag, hashed parental PIN, My List, reminders, ratings, watch progress and subscription state. A phone-verified viewer can add or update a contact email in Account; the one-time link expires after one hour. The pending address and token hash are deleted when confirmed or when delivery fails; unconfirmed rows are purged one day after expiry. A request timestamp enforces the one-minute resend limit and is cleared one day later. On an initial add, the server retains the internal placeholder until confirmation; on an update, the current confirmed address remains active. Only after the link is redeemed does the server promote the pending address for account/billing email.
- **SMS:** MSG91 receives the normalized phone number and six-digit sign-in code for delivery. MySQL stores the phone number on the account and a SHA-256 code hash, expiry and attempts (not the code itself). OTP rows expire ten minutes after issuance and the scheduler removes them one day after expiry. Account deletion removes OTP rows for that account phone.
- **Support/contact:** tickets and replies can hold name, email, optional phone, category, subject/body, app version, platform and device details. The server contact inbox stores name, email, optional phone and message. If the site runs without its API, its contact form sends the same fields to the configured Google Forms endpoint instead; that copy is outside the account database. If `CONTACT_WEBHOOK_URL` is configured, the server also POSTs submissions to the operator-selected endpoint (for example, Slack/Zapier/Apps Script).
- **Billing:** payment rows retain plan, amount/currency, status and Razorpay order/payment IDs. Refund rows can retain provider refund ID, amount, status and a recorded reason. Invoice/credit-note snapshots may retain buyer name/email, GSTIN/state, tax amounts and payment/refund references; new SMS-only-account invoices omit the internal placeholder as a buyer email. Receipts, refund notices, expiry reminders and emailed documents are not sent to the placeholder. Until a phone-only account confirms a contact email, its documents remain downloadable and refund status is shown in Billing; after confirmation, eligible future billing emails go to that address. Historical invoice snapshots are not rewritten. The server does not store card numbers, UPI credentials or bank-account credentials; Razorpay processes checkout/payment details.
- **Devices and diagnostics:** push tokens/subscriptions and preferences, playback device ID/label/video/recent activity, and client/server error details (which may include account ID, URL, user-agent, error/stack). Aggregate play counts and watch-time statistics are not tied to a profile.
- **Local mode:** without the API, guest profiles, ratings, lists, progress and preferences can stay in local browser/app storage. These are not uploaded to the account database and are not removed by a server-side account deletion.

## Providers and optional processing

Depending on the feature and deployment, the service uses Razorpay, MSG91, the configured SMTP/email provider, the hosting/database provider, Cloudflare R2, Google/Facebook/Apple sign-in, YouTube embeds, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Forms for the no-API contact fallback, an optional operator-selected contact webhook when configured, and Google Analytics only after consent. YouTube and third-party sign-in/push providers may collect connection/device data under their own policies. Confirm the actual providers and processing locations configured in production; “optional” integrations still need to be represented accurately in the stores' Data safety answers.

## Google Play Data safety — review for every release

This repository does not contain or update the Play Console form. The Play Console owner must submit answers that match every distributed app version, bundled SDK, production feature and provider configuration. Use the current Play Console definitions for “collected,” “shared,” purposes and optional collection rather than copying a static checkbox list. In particular, do not reuse the old short description that listed only email/name and watch history or claimed “no tracking.”

Use this code-and-provider map as the review checklist:

- **Personal information:** account name, email (including a pending user-supplied address collected for confirmation), verified phone number used for SMS sign-in, profile names, and support/contact details. A phone-only account keeps an internal, non-contactable placeholder email until confirmation; the pending address is used for the confirmation flow and billing/account mail only after confirmation.
- **App activity / purchase history:** profiles, ratings, My List, reminders, watch progress, subscription state, payment and refund history, and invoice/credit-note details. Invoice snapshots can include name, GSTIN/state, amounts and payment/refund references. The app/server do not store card numbers, UPI credentials or bank-account credentials; Razorpay processes checkout details.
- **Messages and diagnostics:** support tickets/replies and contact-form submissions; client/server error reports may contain an account identifier, URL, user-agent, error and stack details. The service also holds administrator audit metadata, including IP addresses, for security/accountability.
- **Device or other IDs:** app push tokens sent to Firebase Cloud Messaging, browser push endpoints, and playback device IDs/labels. Check the diagnostics and network logs provided by the deployed hosting/database providers as well.
- **Provider/SDK review:** MSG91 handles phone numbers and OTP delivery; SMTP handles email confirmation and account/billing messages; Firebase handles app push; Razorpay handles checkout/payment data; Google/Facebook/Apple handle chosen sign-in; YouTube is contacted when its player/media is loaded; Google Analytics is enabled only after consent; Google Forms and a contact webhook are deployment-dependent alternatives/copies. Hosting, database, and media providers process service data. Decide “shared”/service-provider answers from the actual contract and Play's current definitions, not from the fact that a provider is optional.
- **Deletion and retention:** disclose the in-app and external account-deletion paths and the legitimate records that survive detached from an account (billing, audit, backups, external/provider copies). See the retention details below. Account deletion is not a promise that provider copies or pre-existing backups are erased immediately.

Before release, inspect the production environment, the exact Android bundle and third-party SDK list, verify consent-gated analytics behavior, and compare the completed Play Console form with the public policy and `/delete-account`. Update the Play Console entry outside this code change; merely changing this guide does not change Google's published declaration.

## Account deletion and retention implemented in code

- **Self-service and external request:** signed-in users can delete under Account → Delete account (`DELETE /api/v1/me`). The public, crawlable resource is `/delete-account`, linked from the footer and the Privacy Policy; it gives a pre-addressed email request for people who cannot sign in. The request must be verified before deletion. Both paths should remain working and easy to find.
- **Removed at account deletion:** account and social identities, profiles and their ratings/library/reminders/progress, subscription, credits/referrals, auth tokens, pending email-change tokens and addresses, linked push registrations, account-phone OTP rows, linked refund requests, error reports, playback sessions, automatic-notification ledger rows, and support tickets/replies linked by account ID, account email or normalized verified phone number. Pending email rows are also protected by a user foreign key with `ON DELETE CASCADE`. Contact messages in the server database matching the account email or normalized verified phone number are removed. App push is opted out on the requesting installation before deletion so the client does not immediately register that token as a guest.
- **Billing retained:** payment, refund and invoice/credit-note rows survive detached from the account; invoice/tax snapshots can still contain buyer name/email, GSTIN/state, payment/refund references, amounts, and credit-note reason. No billing purge job is configured, so do not promise an exact deletion date or say the rows are automatically deleted after a fixed statutory period. Razorpay may also retain its own records.
- **Broadcast history:** on deletion, campaign delivery `user_id`, name, email, destination and error are cleared, and the account-linked delivery digest is replaced with a fresh random key; campaign counts/status remain until a completed campaign is pruned after 365 days. A matching campaign creator email is cleared.
- **Automatic schedules:** support tickets are pruned 365 days after last activity; server contact messages 365 days after submission; error reports after 30 days; playback sessions after 90 days of inactivity; expired OTP hashes one day after expiry; pending email-change links expire after one hour, unconfirmed address/token rows and rate-limit timestamps are purged one day after expiry/request; inactive native app push tokens after 180 days. Scheduled jobs invoke these pruners.
- **Backups:** account deletion does not rewrite existing database backups. The backup script rotates local files to the newest 14 by default when it runs (`BACKUP_KEEP` is configurable); copies uploaded to a separate R2 bucket and hosting/database snapshots are not pruned by account deletion and follow separate operator/provider retention. Backup encryption is optional and depends on `BACKUP_PASSPHRASE`.
- **Audit and outside copies:** `admin_audit` is append-only and has no automatic retention. It can contain an administrator email, action details/metadata, target account email or UUID, and IP address; older deletion audit rows may still have a target email. Google Forms submissions, contact-webhook recipient copies, SMTP/mailbox copies, provider/security logs and local-only data on a user's device are not automatically deleted from those systems by `DELETE /me`; disclose that limitation and process separate requests where access is available.

The detailed user-facing wording is in `app/js/legal-text.js`; keep it aligned with schema changes, background jobs, and deployed provider configuration. The privacy policy and external deletion resource must be publicly accessible, non-editable by users, linked in-app, and match the Play Console/App Store privacy answers.

## Other launch checks

- India privacy: publish the verified business/data-fiduciary identity and grievance contact, confirm applicable notice/consent and child-user obligations with counsel, and make sure vendor agreements and retention periods match production.
- App stores: Apple requires Sign in with Apple alongside other third-party sign-in options when applicable. The apps are consumption-only for paid plans and intentionally do not sell or link to website checkout; confirm the current platform/payment-program rules before submission. See `docs/PAYMENTS.md`.
- SMS: DLT registration and approved templates are required for production SMS in India; see `docs/MSG91.md`.
- **No DRM:** premium video is protected by login, paid plan and short-lived signed URLs, which stops casual sharing but not deliberate screen recording or downloading a stream. Studio-grade DRM (Widevine/FairPlay) needs a DRM provider.
- **No offline downloads:** not offered for free YouTube videos (YouTube's terms forbid it) or premium titles (would need DRM).

## GST

Invoices are GST-inclusive at 18% (SAC 998439) with gapless numbering and credit notes — see `docs/BILLING.md`. **Not included:** e-invoice/IRN generation (needed above the turnover threshold; requires a GST Suvidha Provider), GSTR-1/3B filing. The CSV register in Admin → Payments is meant to be handed to your accountant.
